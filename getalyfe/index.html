GetALyf

Don't be a zombie. GetALyf watches your screen time and roasts you with a cartoon of you as one of six lazy archetypes.

How it works

⏱️ Set a threshold

Pick the apps that eat your day. Every X minutes on them, GetALyf pokes you back to real life.

🧟 Six lazy archetypes

Lazy Leer, The Sloth, The Gargoyle, Couch Potato, Doom Drool, and The 3AM Goblin — all drawn as you.

📸 One selfie, once

Setup takes one photo. Google Gemini draws your portraits. Only the drawings are kept, on your phone.

🔒 Nothing leaves after setup

No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Apple counts your minutes on-device.

📊 30-day stats

See how many nudges you racked up and which apps earned them.

💸 No subscription

Starter pack is free. Style packs and regen credits are one-time buys.

Support

Questions, bugs, or feature requests? Email support@getalyf.app. Include your iOS version and a rough description of what happened — one indie dev answering, so keep it kind and I'll answer fast.

Portraits didn't generate? Wait a few minutes — the first pack runs through Google Gemini and takes ~30 seconds per pose. If it still hasn't finished, tap Regenerate from a new selfie on the home screen.

Screen Time permission denied by accident? Grant it in Settings → Screen Time → Share Across Devices → Apps with Screen Time Access → GetALyf.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-28

GetALyf is designed to work almost entirely on your device. This policy is short because we collect almost nothing.

What stays on your phone. Your screen-time usage data, the apps you selected to count toward your threshold, the generated portraits, and all app preferences. This is read via Apple's Family Controls / DeviceActivity frameworks and never leaves your device.

What we send once. When you provide a selfie for portrait generation, that single image is sent to Google's Gemini API through our own server. Google returns cartoon drawings, which are stored on your phone. The selfie itself is not retained by us or by Google (per Google's Gemini API Additional Terms) once the drawings are generated.

What we don't do. No user accounts. No analytics. No advertising. No tracking. No crash reporting. No third-party SDKs beyond the direct call to Google Gemini for portrait generation.

In-app purchases are handled by Apple's App Store. We receive an anonymized purchase receipt only to unlock the content you paid for; we never see your Apple ID, name, email, or payment method.

Children. GetALyf does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy ever changes, the update will appear here with a new date.
