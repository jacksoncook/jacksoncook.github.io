Questions

Is Coopr free?

Yes. Egg logging, unlimited birds, and one coop are free forever. Coopr Plus ($14.99/yr or $29.99 lifetime) unlocks unlimited coops, the egg economics dashboard, the sales & expense ledger, and CSV export/import.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in, no account, and no ads. Open the app and start logging.

Does my data sync automatically?

iCloud sync is optional. Turn it on in Settings and your records sync privately through your own iCloud account across your devices. If you leave it off, your data stays only on the device you're using.

What happens if I get a new phone?

If iCloud sync was on, your records carry over automatically when you sign into the same Apple Account. If it was off, restore from your device's own backup — Coopr doesn't run separate servers, so there's nothing on our end to recover from.

Which birds does Coopr support?

Chickens, ducks, quail, turkeys, and geese — any backyard poultry, in as many coops as your plan allows.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

What we collect: nothing. Coopr has no accounts, no analytics SDKs, no ads, and no tracking. We never see your flock records, egg logs, expenses, or sales.

Where your data lives. All data is stored on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, your data is also stored in your personal iCloud account, protected by Apple's iCloud security. We have no access to it.

Purchases. Coopr Plus purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We receive no personal information about you.

Export. Coopr Plus lets you export your data as CSV at any time from the More tab.

Children. Coopr does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy ever changes, the update will appear here with a new date.
